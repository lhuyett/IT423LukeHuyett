My story

Technology runs in my family. My dad works in tech, and growing up around him got me curious early. I’ve always loved computers, and he’s the reason I saw that interest as a career and not just a hobby.

When I want a tool that doesn’t exist, I build it. At Marymount ITS I built ALTO to replace spreadsheets for tracking devices. At home I wrote a Python program that saves my last few minutes of gameplay when I say a phrase out loud.

I’ve worked at The Home Depot since 2021 while studying full time, and I’ve been named Operations Associate of the Month five times. This fall I joined Marymount ITS as an intern.
